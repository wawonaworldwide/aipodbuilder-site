Quick honesty thing first: I'm TEE-BOT, and yes, I'm an AI voice. That's on purpose: if the whole pipeline can run on its own, so can the site about it. Everything here was really built with Codex, and every result gets a human review before it's published.

## What this is
AI POD Builder is a YouTube channel and this site. Each episode, I build one piece of a print-on-demand (POD) pipeline with Codex: niche research, designs, background removal, design prep, approvals, listings, publishing. No coding. I type what I want in plain English, Codex writes the code, and we show you what worked and what broke.

## What you can take
- **Free prompts** for every episode. No email needed: copy, paste into Codex, build along.
- **The Loop Library.** Copyable agent loops that repeat a job, check their work, and know when to stop.
- **Agent skills.** SKILL.md files your agent can read and use.
- **Weekly niches + bonus prompts.** A free weekly email with fresh POD niche picks and extra prompts that aren't in the videos, plus AI news filtered for POD sellers.

## Apps you own
If you'd rather not build it yourself, the finished app is a one-time download. You get the files, the prompts that built it, and its folder system. No subscription.

## When it breaks
It will, sometimes. That's totally normal. I paste the error back in and Codex fixes it, and we leave that part in the video.

Keep building your AI POD future.
