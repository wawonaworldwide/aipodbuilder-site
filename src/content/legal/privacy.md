## Newsletter information

When you sign up, we send your email address, optional name, signup page and selected preferences to Kit, our email provider. Kit sends a confirmation email. Your subscription uses double opt-in: you must click the confirmation link before receiving subscribed updates. Weekly niche ideas, app updates and launch coupons are separate choices; choosing app alerts does not subscribe you to weekly emails.

Every marketing email includes Kit's unsubscribe link and our mailing address. You can unsubscribe in one click or contact us for help. Kit maintains subscription and suppression information so we can respect your choices and avoid emailing people who have opted out. Ask us to access, correct or delete your subscription information; we may retain information needed to honor an opt-out or a legal obligation.

## Hosting and bot protection

Cloudflare hosts the site and provides Turnstile. It processes technical request information needed to deliver and protect the service. Signup rate limiting uses a hash derived from the request's IP address, with a ten-minute rate-limit window. We do not use those records for advertising.

## Browser settings and analytics

Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage. Cloudflare Web Analytics is currently disabled. We do not use advertising pixels on this site.

## Payments and sharing

Checkout and app downloads are disabled for this launch. No payment details are collected through the site. If purchases are enabled later, this policy will be updated to explain the payment and delivery providers and record retention.

We do not sell personal information. We share it with service providers such as Kit and Cloudflare to operate subscriptions, hosting and security, and where required by law. Their processing may occur outside your country.

## Questions and changes

Contact us at the address above about this policy or your information. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location; nothing here limits rights you have under applicable law. We will post changes here with an updated date.
